Products

Standard hospital gowns are designed for clinical utility — not for the person wearing them. MEND recovery gowns are made from a soft cotton-merino blend, with full-length sleeves, an attached hood, and a single full-length zip — front or back, your choice — made from repurposed bamboo instead of metal. Shoulder-seam zips give clinicians IV access without removing the gown, so there are no ties and no open back. They're machine washable, come in nine colors and patterns, and feel like loungewear, not a paper sheet.
Metal zippers and hardware are a known hazard around MRI machines and other strong magnets. Ferromagnetic metal can turn into a fast-moving projectile, metal threads or hardware can heat up and cause burns during a scan, and metal parts can shift inside the machine's field or interfere with implanted devices like pacemakers. MEND's zippers — and all hardware across our apparel — are made from repurposed bamboo instead, so you can wear MEND apparel through imaging, procedures, and everyday care without giving MRI safety a second thought.

MEND Compression Socks provide 20–30 mmHg graduated compression — the clinical standard for nurses, caregivers, and patients on their feet all day or recovering from surgery. This level is appropriate for most adults and is often recommended by physicians for DVT prevention and edema management. Always consult your doctor if you have a diagnosed circulatory condition.
The standard behind the number. 20–30 mmHg is the pressure measured at the ankle on a graduated profile, the band labelled medical Class 1 under RAL-GZ 387/1. In the US, hosiery for general medical purposes is a Class I device under 21 CFR 880.5780 and exempt from premarket notification. We do not sell or test these for prevention of blood pooling in the legs, which is the Class II use under the same regulation.
It names the standard a device is engineered to meet, and nothing beyond that. MEND Pulse is designed to ISO 81060-2, the international protocol for validating automated blood-pressure monitors, which sets the ±3 mmHg limit. MEND Oxi is designed to ISO 80601-2-61, which defines the ARMS accuracy measure used for pulse oximeters. Every connected device on this site is pending FDA 510(k) clearance and none has completed its clinical validation study yet, so we use clinical-grade to describe the design target rather than a result already achieved. AirGuard™ Safety Collar

AirGuard™ uses a 6-axis IMU (accelerometer + gyroscope) to read the motion signature of a fall — distinct from everyday movement like sitting down quickly, bending over, or bumping into furniture — and triggers in under a second. There's no button to press and nothing to configure.
AirGuard™ automatically sends an SMS and places a call to up to three designated emergency contacts, sharing your location. No app needs to be open, and there's no button to press — the alert goes out on its own.
That's what the 6-axis sensor is built for — distinguishing an actual fall from things like walking on uneven terrain, light exercise, or household tasks. We haven't published clinical trial figures yet, since the device is still pending FDA 510(k) clearance ahead of launch.

Sizing & Fit

MEND gowns are sized S–XL and scrubs are sized XS–XL, both true to standard US sizing. For gowns, we recommend ordering your usual top size for the best fit. Compression socks are sized by shoe size rather than measurement: S/M (shoe 5–9), M/L (shoe 8–11), L/XL (shoe 10–13).
Gowns and scrubs currently top out at XL. We believe recovery wear should fit every body, so if you need sizing outside our standard range, please contact us — we accommodate custom sizing for bulk facility orders and are evaluating extended sizing for a future release.

Care Instructions

Machine wash cold, gentle cycle, with like colors. Tumble dry low or lay flat to dry. Do not bleach. Do not iron directly on embroidered areas. Our cotton-merino blend is designed to maintain softness and shape through repeated washing — tested to 100+ wash cycles with no significant pilling or shrinkage.
AirGuard™ is IP67 water-resistant, so you can shower, wash your hands, or get caught in the rain without taking it off. Wipe the silicone band clean with a damp cloth as needed — it's not designed to be machine washed or submerged.
