Products

Standard hospital gowns are designed for clinical utility — not for the person wearing them. MEND recovery gowns are made from a soft cotton-merino blend, close fully at the back with a dignified design, and feature short sleeves with a front zip — made from repurposed bamboo instead of metal — for IV and catheter access. They're machine washable, available in multiple colors, and built to feel like loungewear, not a paper sheet.
Metal zippers and hardware are a known hazard around MRI machines and other strong magnets. Ferromagnetic metal can turn into a fast-moving projectile, metal threads or hardware can heat up and cause burns during a scan, and metal parts can shift inside the machine's field or interfere with implanted devices like pacemakers. MEND's zippers — and most metal hardware across our apparel — are made from repurposed bamboo instead, so you can wear MEND products through imaging, procedures, and everyday care without giving MRI safety a second thought.

AirGuard™ Safety Collar

AirGuard™ uses a 6-axis IMU (accelerometer + gyroscope) to read the motion signature of a fall — distinct from everyday movement like sitting down quickly, bending over, or bumping into furniture — and triggers in under a second. There's no button to press and nothing to configure.
AirGuard™ automatically sends an SMS and places a call to up to three designated emergency contacts, sharing your location. No app needs to be open, and there's no button to press — the alert goes out on its own.

Sizing & Fit

MEND gowns are sized S–XL and scrubs are sized XS–XL, both true to standard US sizing. For gowns, we recommend ordering your usual top size for the best fit. Compression socks are sized by shoe size rather than measurement: S/M (shoe 5–9), M/L (shoe 8–11), L/XL (shoe 10–13).
Gowns and scrubs currently top out at XL. We believe recovery wear should fit every body, so if you need sizing outside our standard range, please contact us — we accommodate custom sizing for bulk facility orders and are evaluating extended sizing for a future release.

Care Instructions

Machine wash cold, gentle cycle, with like colors. Tumble dry low or lay flat to dry. Do not bleach. Do not iron directly on embroidered areas. Our cotton-merino blend is designed to maintain softness and shape through repeated washing — tested to 100+ wash cycles with no significant pilling or shrinkage.
AirGuard™ is IP67 water-resistant, so you can shower, wash your hands, or get caught in the rain without taking it off. Wipe the silicone band clean with a damp cloth as needed — it's not designed to be machine washed or submerged.
