AirGuard™ is a lightweight wearable collar designed for older adults living independently. It detects the distinct motion signature of a fall the instant it happens — not minutes later — and immediately alerts a designated caregiver or emergency contact. No button to press. No app to open. Just quiet, constant protection that disappears into daily life until it's needed.

How It Works

01

Wear it

Slip AirGuard™ on like a soft collar. It sits comfortably at the base of the neck — slim enough to tuck under a shirt, discreet enough to forget it's there.

02

It senses a fall

The 6-axis sensor reads the unique motion signature of a fall — not a stumble, not sitting down fast — and triggers in under a second.

03

Help arrives

An automated alert goes to up to three emergency contacts via SMS and call. Location is shared. No button to press. No app. No delay.

About AirGuard™

AirGuard™ is the fourth product in the MEND line — built on the same core belief as Comfort Wrap and Comfort Scrubs: that the medical world doesn't have to feel clinical and cold. Falls are the leading cause of injury-related death in adults over 65. Most happen alone. Most go unanswered for too long.

AirGuard™ doesn't ask the wearer to do anything. It just listens, and when the moment comes, it acts. So people can stay independent longer — and the people who love them can worry a little less.
