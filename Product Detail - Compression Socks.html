Track-Resistant Webbing — What It Is

The cuff that doesn't give up.

Most compression socks use a soft rolled elastic band at the top — it looks fine on day one and loses its shape by month two. Track-Resistant Webbing is different by design.

It's a heavy-duty, high-tensile polyester strap woven directly into the cuff structure — the same material used in sports gear, pet harnesses, and industrial tie-downs. Dense, abrasion-resistant, built to hold its shape through hundreds of hot-wash cycles.

  • Won't roll, bunch, or migrate down the calf mid-shift
  • Resists abrasion, tearing, and friction against scrub fabric
  • Hot-dye custom imprint — MEND branding that doesn't wash or peel off
  • Tubular weave distributes compression evenly around the cuff
  • Pairs with the sock's graduated zones for consistent hold all day
