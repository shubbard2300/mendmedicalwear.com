Questions Facilities Ask

Is there a minimum order?

Not to request pilot pricing. Custom logo embroidery starts at 24 units per SKU per colorway.

When do we pay?

Nothing is due until you approve a pre-production sample. We'll confirm quantities, sizes, and pricing in writing before anything is invoiced.

How do the gowns hold up to laundering?

They're designed for 100+ warm wash cycles. Wash testing runs on our first production samples, and pilot facilities get those results as they come in.

Are the gowns MRI-compatible?

The apparel contains no metal: plastic zipper teeth and sliders, resin snaps, bamboo pulls. The garment itself adds no MRI risk. Patients with implanted devices still need your team's usual clearance.

What paperwork can you provide?

A W-9 and product spec sheets come with your quote. Tell us what your supplier onboarding needs and we'll work through it with you.
