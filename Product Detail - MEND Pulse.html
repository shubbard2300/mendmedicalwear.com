On-cuff display — pairing & control

A high-contrast OLED sits right on the cuff, with pairing and controls under your thumb. Readings surface in calm, oversized type that can be read across a room — or without reading glasses.

Integrated air pump

The inflation pump is built into the cuff body and dampened below conversation volume. No hoses, no separate unit — and none of the reading-time anxiety older devices create.

Adjustable Velcro strap

One wide, skin-friendly Velcro strap wraps and snugs the cuff with a single hand — even your non-dominant one. Comfortable on bare skin, precise over 22–42 cm arms.

Lithium-ion battery + Bluetooth

A rechargeable lithium-ion cell runs up to six months per USB-C charge, while Bluetooth 5.3 keeps the cuff paired to the MEND App — hands-off from the day it arrives.
